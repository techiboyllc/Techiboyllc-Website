Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. Techiboy LLC operates techiboyllc.com and is committed to protecting any information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, place an order, or subscribe to our communications. This may include:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, and business name.
  • Project Information: Brand details, industry, preferences, and any files or assets you share for your logo design project.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely through third-party payment processors. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our website — collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver and manage your logo design and branding projects.
  • Communicate updates, proofs, and revisions related to your order.
  • Process payments and send invoices or receipts.
  • Improve our website experience and service quality.
  • Send occasional promotional emails (you may opt out at any time).
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Techiboy LLC does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating our business — such as payment processors, email platforms, and cloud storage — solely to fulfill our services. These parties are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and remember your preferences. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function properly. By continuing to use our site, you consent to our use of cookies.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures — including SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted access protocols — to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@techiboyllc.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please reach out to: Techiboy LLC — Email: support@techiboyllc.com — Website: techiboyllc.com
