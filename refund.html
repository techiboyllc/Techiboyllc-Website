Refund Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsRefund Policy

We stand behind our work with a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not happy with the results, we will make it right — or refund you. Here is exactly how it works.

1. Our Satisfaction Guarantee

Techiboy LLC is committed to delivering logo designs you love. We offer unlimited revisions within your package until you are satisfied. If after completing all revision rounds you are still not satisfied, you may be eligible for a refund under the conditions below.

2. Full Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund before any design concepts have been delivered to you.
  • You request a refund within 24 hours of placing your order and no work has commenced.
  • We are unable to deliver your project within twice the stated turnaround time due to our own delay.

3. Partial Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a partial refund if:

  • Initial concepts have been delivered but no revisions have been requested — a 50% refund may be issued.
  • Revisions are in progress but the project is less than 50% complete as determined by Techiboy LLC — a partial refund may be negotiated.

4. No Refund Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The final logo has been approved and all files have been delivered.
  • The client has used the logo commercially in any form.
  • Revisions were completed and the client did not respond within the 14-day feedback window.
  • The refund request is based on a change of mind after all revision rounds are exhausted.
  • The client provided incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading project brief information.

5. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund request, please contact us at support@techiboyllc.com with your order details and reason for the request. We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method.

6. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Unauthorized chargebacks on completed work will be disputed and may result in being barred from future services. We are always willing to resolve issues fairly — please reach out first.

7. Contact Us

Techiboy LLC — Email: support@techiboyllc.com — Website: techiboyllc.com. We are here to help — our goal is always a happy client and a great logo.
